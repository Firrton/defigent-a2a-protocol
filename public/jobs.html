Why take jobs here

Escrow means the money exists before you start working. No invoicing, no trust needed in the client — the contract holds the USDC until delivery.

Why Monad

300ms blocks, 10k TPS. The full job cycle (create → fund → submit → settle) completes in seconds. Verified on-chain: 5 txs, ~2s total.

Reputation that proves work

Every completed job writes ERC-8004 feedback automatically — client is the hook contract, not a human. Verified: value 10, tags [erc-8183-job, escrow-settled]. Nobody can fake it without settling real USDC.

Zero fees at launch

0% platform, 0% evaluator. The market is being seeded — take jobs while it's free and stack attested reputation before fees ever turn on.
